CHEN NOTATION AND SCOPE
Rectangles: entity types. Diamonds: relationship types. Ellipses: selected attributes. Underlining: primary-key attributes; ExperimentSample has a three-part composite key. Dashed ellipse: derived assay_count = COUNT(SampleTest rows for that sample), not a stored column.
1 and N denote maximum cardinality. Double lines mean total participation (required FK) on the child side; single lines mean partial participation. Parent participation is partial: a parent may have no referring records.
HAS_USAGE relationship attributes quantity_used map to ExperimentSample on the N side. HAS_TEST requested_at maps to SampleTest.
Associative/event entities preserve distinct usage roles and repeated events. SampleRelationship has two named roles to Sample; this reifies the recursive derivation relationship without incorrectly collapsing repeated events.
AncientSpecimen uses the inherited Sample key; EXTENDS is 1:1 with total participation by AncientSpecimen and partial participation by Sample. It is not labelled a weak entity merely because its PK is also an FK.
No weak, composite or multivalued attribute is invented to imitate the textbook sample. Full physical schemas contain additional descriptive/security attributes not shown in this selected-attribute conceptual diagram.
The 11-table model excludes ArchaeologicalSite; its outside-scope FK is explicitly listed below. Both diagrams omit RestrictedMaterial, AIAnalysis, ModelRegistry and AuditLog by the agreed selected scope.
